# Research candidates from Sophon

Reviewed November 21, 2026. The Sophon directory supplied 630 candidate records. It includes model-card citations, task sets, environments, ranking sites and alternate entries for the same evaluation. We traced the research, finance, legal, data-analysis and retrieval candidates to the producers that report the scores. The directory's provider and benchmark counts describe discovery coverage. They do not measure how many configurations can enter ProChoice. [Sophon directory](https://sophon.at/evals)

## Workbook construction and data integration

**Excel Modeling v1.1, Scratch mode.** SpreadsheetBench led us to investigate benchmarks where the model must return a functioning workbook. Vals has 51 private expert case specifications. Each case runs twice, once with an empty workbook and once with a template. Only the Scratch score enters Research. Its rubric checks financial values, formulas and presentation. The evaluator publishes 48 Scratch scores. On November 21, 28 rows passed ProChoice's identity, effort and fallback checks across nine providers. All 28 had reported SE. Scores and costs come from the Scratch partition. Claude Fable 5.2, for example, scored 73.625 there, 81.388 in Template, and 77.377 overall. These are three summaries of the same case set. [Vals scoring method and release](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/emb-v1-1) · [Sophon spreadsheet entry](https://sophon.at/evals/spreadsheetbench-verified)

## Further candidates

- **SAGE.** Sophon labels it Legal. The producer evaluates agreement with expert graders on handwritten student math assessments. The current release has 30 model rows, and its publishing pipeline includes effort and SE. Grading a student's work constitutes a separate educational task outside this index. [Sophon entry](https://sophon.at/evals/vals-sage) · [SAGE](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/sage)
- **SpreadsheetBench.** The verified 400-task v1 board has 18 product or agent rows. On v2, only eight of 11 rows use SWE-agent. Five names from that slice resolve to providers in the model registry, before effort matching. The export has no effort or per-row uncertainty field. The full v1, verified v1 and v2 tables cannot supply comparable independent measurements. [Producer leaderboard](https://spreadsheetbench.github.io/) · [V1 verified data](https://spreadsheetbench.github.io/data/leaderboard-v1-verified.json) · [V2 results](https://spreadsheetbench.github.io/data/leaderboard-v2-full.json)
- **BIRD Mini-Dev.** The October baseline has nine configurations across five providers, measured on SQLite, MySQL and PostgreSQL. The three dialects use the same 500 aligned question IDs. The published fixed-prompt table has no run-level reasoning effort or reported sampling uncertainty. The larger leaderboard accepts different SQL agents and self-consistency settings. Per-run configuration evidence would make the 500-task fixed-prompt evaluation worth revisiting. [BIRD Mini-Dev dataset and baselines](https://huggingface.co/datasets/birdsql/bird_mini_dev) · [BIRD leaderboard](https://bird-bench.github.io/)
- **ScholarSearch.** The OpenReward version has 223 scholarly QA entries. Its grader checks an answer in one turn, with no search tools. It can assess knowledge of a publication, but cannot demonstrate the quality of web retrieval or citation checking. [OpenReward environment](https://openreward.ai/GeneralReasoning/ScholarSearch)
- **MarketBench.** The publisher asks for three trading backtesters with verifiable outputs. Its visible market index covers three models, below the five-provider calibration minimum, and its result measures backtesting implementation. [MarketBench](https://marketbench.ai/)
- **TaxEval v2, MortgageTax and Case Law v2.** Their producer explicitly labels these benchmarks archived due to saturation. They list 145, 98 and 54 results respectively. Persistent historical rows alone cannot establish current frontier discrimination. [TaxEval](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/tax_eval_v2) · [MortgageTax](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/mortgage_tax) · [Case Law](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/case_law_v2)
- **FrontierScience.** OpenAI's gold set separates 60 research tasks from 100 Olympiad tasks. A pooled score would repeat scientific problem solving already represented by Terminal-Bench-Science and mathematical reasoning covered by FrontierMath. The release table also compares differing model configurations. [Producer report](https://openai.com/index/frontierscience/)

The private case banks do not permit an independent item-by-item intersection. These decisions use the producers' documented inputs, required work, task identifiers where public, artifacts and grading rules. Interpreting two related signals as separate tests requires evidence of a different assignment. Changing the judge, feed or version alone does not establish it.
