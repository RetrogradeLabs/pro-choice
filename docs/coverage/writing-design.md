# Writing and design coverage

## Current v3 roster

Writing scores four outputs. Hemingway measures short commissioned pieces, EQ Longform a planned eight-chapter manuscript, WritingBench its Academic & Engineering briefs only, and MedScribe a structured note from a clinical transcript. Lech Mazur and Arena Creative Writing remain standalone measures of short creative prose. [Current source decisions and matched models](sophon-writing-design.md)

Design scores a web interface from Arena WebDev, a vector graphic from DesignArena SVG, a formatted document or presentation from LiveBench Design, and a rendered product or brand video from Code2Video-AA. LiveBench checks Word, PDF and slide files against the requested brief. Code2Video renders HyperFrames HTML and GSAP timelines before its judge compares the resulting videos; using HTML in that pipeline does not turn it into a web-interface evaluation. DesignArena publishes SVG-category preference ratings from voters. Some SVGs can include animation. Its feed provides no public list of prompts that would establish zero shared briefs. Vals Vibe Code and DesignArena Website remain standalone alternatives for web work. [LiveBench task breakdown](https://livebench.ai/data/20261106/breakdown.json) · [Code2Video evaluation](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/code2video-aa) · [SVG score feed](https://www.designarena.ai/api/leaderboard?arenaType=models&category=svg)

| Source | Evidence used | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [WritingBench](https://huggingface.co/spaces/WritingBench/WritingBench) | The authors' overall score covers 1,000 queries across six domains. | [Hemingway-bench](https://surgehq.ai/benchmarks/hemingway-bench) | Professional writers compare creative, business and everyday writing. | [Arena Creative Writing](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset) | Style-controlled human preferences on creative-writing prompts. | The same text dataset also labels battles by industry, language and difficulty. Adding its business and legal industry slices would reuse votes and include tasks other than writing. |
| [Lech Mazur](https://github.com/lechmazur/writing) | Evaluators compare stories written to matched briefs in both orders. The leaderboard publishes bootstrap intervals over stories and evaluators. | Counts differ by model. The source documents incomplete stories, length redraws and refusal retries. Its score describes the completed stories. |
| [EQ-Bench Longform](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing_longform.html) | Protocol 1.11 grades a model's plan, eight chapters and completed story using Claude Sonnet 4.6. V2 used the overall longform writing score. | Twelve distinct story prompts generate the dependent chapters. Counting 96 independent observations would understate the sampling error. [Published prompts](https://github.com/EQ-bench/longform-writing-bench/blob/main/data/longform_creative_writing_prompts_minimalist.json) |

WritingBench also appears in independent comparisons of writing systems. Their scores use their own evaluation conditions. Pooling their tables with the authors' board would confound model changes with judge or protocol changes. ## Other sources screened

| Source | Decision and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| [PLawBench](https://github.com/skylenage/PLawbench) | Task 3 grades legal complaints and statements of defense. Three of the 24 published model rows resolved to registered configurations across two labs in this audit. The score cannot clear the five-lab linking gate. The overall would also count legal consultation and case analysis. [ACL 2026 paper](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.458) |
| [EQ-Bench Creative Writing v3](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html) | A separate short-form benchmark with 32 prompts and three repeated generations. The published headline Elo feed lacks the paired interval required for admission. Its rubric score helped initialize Elo. Counting both scores would reuse the same outputs. [Protocol](https://eqbench.com/about.html#creative-writing-v3) |
| [Harvey LAB-AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/harvey-lab-aa), [AA-Briefcase](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-briefcase), [GDPval-AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdpval-aa) | Keep the professional-work headline scores in Research. A legal deliverable can fail a substantive criterion despite good prose. Briefcase and GDPval mix formats and occupations, with no general-purpose writing-only headline. |
| [DesignArena Slides](https://www.designarena.ai/api/leaderboard?arenaType=models&category=slides), [PPTAgent](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.728) | DesignArena's 13 entries are products or harness configurations, including Gamma and Claude Code. PPTAgent measures an editing workflow. Assigning either score to its underlying language model would misattribute the result. |
| [AA-3D](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-3d) | Measures production of interactive three.js scenes. None of its 27 chart entries had a positive 95% interval half-width of at most 25 Elo on the review date. Recheck when the publisher updates the intervals. |
| [CAD Eval](https://willpatrick.xyz/cadevalresults_20250422_095709/), [SpatialViz-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07610) | CAD Eval scores geometric fidelity of generated solids on 50 tasks using an April 2025 model panel. SpatialViz tests mental rotation, folding, penetration and animation. Neither measures the quality of a visual communication artifact made to a brief. |
| [Arena Text-to-Image](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset/tree/main/text_to_image) | Rows identify image-generation models such as GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst. Their scores and per-image prices cannot be assigned to a text-model SKU on the design frontier. Raster image generation remains outside this index. |

These decisions apply to the published protocols and model panels above. Source revisions, judge changes and newly attributable scores can change them. New model evaluations can enter an existing protocol through daily ingestion. A different protocol or a change in the active roster requires a new major epoch.
