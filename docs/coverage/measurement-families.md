# The scored roster and its target

ProChoice scores 27 instruments in six domain composites. Each measures one retained kind of work in that domain, with a declared underlying dataset or task split. Other useful measurements remain available as standalone observations. Their scores, costs and uncertainty do not enter the composite.

| --- | --- |
| Coding, 5 | FrontierCode-AA 2.0 for repository changes, Vals Code Migration for ports across languages, MirrorCode for behavioral reconstruction, ALE-Bench for algorithm optimization, ITBench for incident diagnosis. |
| Writing, 4 | Hemingway-bench for short-form editorial briefs, EQ-Bench Longform for sustained narrative, the Academic & Engineering split of WritingBench for technical writing, MedScribe for clinical documentation from an encounter. |

| Defensive security, 2 | Aikido for locating defects, CWE-bench v2 for verifying repairs. The v1 mixed competition score remains in the historical archive. |

Controlled harness comparisons are a separate product. One board measures the 17 Terminal-Bench 4.0 tasks sampled by FrontierHarness. Another measures the DeepSWE tasks reported by Harness Leaderboard. Each board compares harnesses only on its own tasks and control models. There is no cross-instrument ranking. The source versions, controls, scored run counts and costs belong to their respective instruments. [Harness and security audit](harnesses-security.md)

## What the overlap check establishes

The registry records the primary work product and stable upstream task pool for every scored input. A domain fails calibration if two active inputs claim the same work family or task pool. An overall and its known subscore must share the subscore's pool identifier. For example, Coding has one repository-change instrument; LiveBench Coding, DeepSWE and SWE-bench Pro are standalone alternatives. LiveBench Table Join can score Research because its parent Data Analysis aggregate is standalone. Design counts one website instrument despite tracking three. [Registry](../../src/config/measurement-families.ts) · [Coding decisions](sophon-coding.md) · [Writing and design decisions](writing-design.md) · [Research decisions](coding-research.md)

Two publishers can evaluate effectively the same work, and one publisher can test two different tasks. A common judge is evidence of possible common error. It does not by itself establish task duplication. The gate checks declared provenance. It cannot prove that private items share no source materials, that all judges make independent errors, or that a score predicts a buyer's particular assignments.

## Weights and uncertainty

Each retained work family contributes one unit to its domain. We have no measured population frequencies for the different kinds of work a buyer might do. Equal weights make the target explicit and keep it independent of the expected model ordering. The separate quality estimate combines provider-balanced reliability with discrimination among leading providers. Sampling uncertainty, sparse links, missing measurements and empirical Bayes shrinkage still affect the published score and interval.

As a prespecified alternative, the audit fits the same model using the frozen quality formula instead. It holds out a whole data feed, estimates anchors, links, difficulty and priors from other feeds, then compares each fit against raw scores on exactly the configurations available to both. A second baseline uses a simple mean of the available standardized results. The daily report includes the matched sample sizes and the per-benchmark correlations. [Lifecycle evidence](../../data/lifecycle.json)

In the November 21 analysis of the 27-instrument roster, the mean within-feed difference in Kendall tau-b, quality minus equal, was −0.0501 for Coding, +0.1428 for Writing, +0.0028 for Design and −0.0060 for Research. Respectively 2, 1, 3 and 10 held-out benchmarks yielded a paired comparison. The two calculable stack comparisons tied. Small matched cohorts and missing scores limit what these differences establish. They did not justify granting a test more domain weight because it more strongly separates these models. The OECD handbook treats the conceptual framework, weighting choice and sensitivity analysis as distinct decisions. Siska et al. show that changing the assumed task distribution can change LLM rankings. [OECD and JRC handbook](https://doi.org/10.1787/9789264043466-en) · [Siska et al., ACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.560/)

For the current scoring epochs, any benchmark covering less than 40% of the broadest calibration panel requires an overlap link. On the full overlap and after dropping any one provider, the provider-balanced correlation with reference ability must stay positive. The archive records the smallest such correlation. A positive association is a minimum condition for linking two scales, not proof that a narrow panel measures the same latent quantity with useful precision. A bounded score with a verified count of independent parent tasks uses its conservative sampling-error bound once in the quality diagnostic. Original v1 and v2 archive calculations retain their published conventions.
