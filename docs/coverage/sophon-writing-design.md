# Writing and design evidence in Sophon

Screened November 21, 2026. Sophon’s catalogue returned 630 records. I inspected the Writing entries and searched the titles and descriptions for professional drafting, creative work, visual artifacts, editing, presentations, web interfaces, diagrams and design. The directory supplied leads. The decisions below use the task producer’s score, protocol and model identifiers. [Sophon evals](https://sophon.at/evals)

## Four writing tasks

| Scored source | Output used in v3 and why it has its own place |
| --- | --- |
| WritingBench Academic & Engineering | The author’s 1,000-query manifest contains **167 different academic and engineering briefs across 17 subdomains**, including abstracts, technical documentation, limitations and research proposals. The November 27, 2025 table publishes a domain score separately for each of its 54 models. Join the domain row to the overall row by model label and verify the overall value before using the organization. Only the academic subscore enters. The authors average the available query scores. Per-model completion counts and uncertainty are absent from the table. [Task manifest](https://github.com/X-PLUG/WritingBench/blob/main/benchmark_query/benchmark_all.jsonl) · [Aggregation](https://github.com/X-PLUG/WritingBench/blob/main/calculate_scores.py) · [Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/WritingBench/WritingBench) |
| Hemingway-bench | Expert writers compare standalone pieces commissioned for everyday, business and creative purposes. The publisher describes landlord emails, business documents and short stories, and publishes 95% intervals for aggregate preferences. This is the single representative for short-form general and creative writing. [Judging protocol](https://surgehq.ai/blog/hemingway-bench-ai-writing-leaderboard) · [Results](https://surgehq.ai/benchmarks/hemingway-bench) |
| EQ-Bench Longform 1.11 | A model develops a plan and writes eight successive chapters. The producer grades the plan, chapters and completed story. This tests continuity through an extended manuscript. Twelve distinct prompts generate the chapters. Repeated chapters cannot supply independent tasks for the uncertainty bound. [Protocol and results](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing_longform.html) |
| Vals MedScribe | Given a simulated consultation transcript, produce a structured SOAP note. The evaluator grades factual documentation against a case-specific reference. Diagnosis and prescribing decisions are outside this writing score. Use the single overall note-quality score with the producer’s configuration, fallback and uncertainty metadata. [Protocol and results](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/medscribe) |

WritingBench has nine registry matches on the review date. Eight also appear on EQ Longform with the same canonical configuration: GPT-5 medium, o3 medium, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Opus 4.5, Haiku 4.5, DeepSeek R1-0528, Gemini 3 Pro high and Llama 4 Scout. They span five providers. The Anthropic matches use the registered no-thinking default. The publisher does not report explicit effort for every row. The retired DeepSeek checkpoint contributes calibration evidence only. A successful link does not establish academic-writing coverage for newer models that never ran these briefs.

## Other writing leads

| Source | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Lech Mazur Creative Story-Writing and Arena Creative Writing | Keep their results inspectable outside the composite. Lech Mazur evaluates short stories produced from shared briefs, with bootstrap uncertainty. Arena collects preferences on creative-writing requests. Hemingway already measures short creative output. Adding all three would let one kind of work carry three votes. [Lech Mazur](https://github.com/lechmazur/writing) · [Arena split](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset) |
| EQ-Bench Creative Writing v3 | Consider as a replacement if short fiction becomes its own track. The published board contains 146 model rows with a rubric score and an Elo score on the same 32 prompts and three generations per prompt. The October 25 protocol names GLM 5.3 as rubric judge and Claude Opus 5 as pairwise judge. The detailed GPT-6 Astra report confirms the rubric judge. The public Elo score table lacks row-level intervals, and the downloadable June run archive uses earlier judges. Either score would bring another short-fiction measure into this index. [Protocol](https://eqbench.com/about.html#creative-writing-v3) · [Results](https://eqbench.com/creative_writing.html) · [Example model report](https://eqbench.com/results/creative-writing-v3/gpt-6-astra.html) · [Run archive](https://github.com/EQ-bench/creative-writing-bench) |
| IFBench, IFEval and LiveBench Instruction Following | Useful proxies for checking formal constraints. IFBench combines 58 new constraint types with user prompts. Its released test file has 300 prompts. A response can satisfy a count or formatting rule while failing to communicate effectively. WritingBench already grades adherence to requirements on the actual academic deliverable. Treat instruction-following results separately. [IFBench data and verifiers](https://github.com/allenai/IFBench) · [IFEval](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/instruction_following_eval) |
| Thematic Generalization | Infer a hidden rule from three examples and three counterexamples, then identify one of eight candidates. The headline 703-item subset is selected using model failures from a 1,247-item pool. The output is a classification, and the Prime environment repackages the task. Exclude both records from Writing. [Producer’s protocol and subsets](https://github.com/lechmazur/generalization) |
| EQ-Bench 4 and SWE Atlas TW | The former grades emotional understanding and roleplay. TW means software test writing. Neither provides a distinct professional prose grade. [EQ-Bench methodology](https://eqbench.com/about.html) · [Scale Test Writing](https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/sweatlas-tw) |

## Design leads

Keep one representative for building a website, Arena WebDev. Vals Vibe Code, which Sophon lists, and DesignArena Website cover substantially the same user task. Their standalone results preserve differences between test criteria and human preferences. The other scored outputs are a vector illustration, a formatted document or presentation, and an animated composition. [Arena WebDev](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset) · [Vals Vibe Code](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/vibe-code) · [DesignArena SVG](https://www.designarena.ai/api/leaderboard?arenaType=models&category=svg) · [LiveBench task breakdown](https://livebench.ai/data/20261106/breakdown.json) · [Code2Video-AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/code2video-aa)

| Additional source | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| BenchCAD | Sophon’s entry describes reconstruction from rendered views. The producer now also offers editing and hardware engineering on a newer task set. Geometric fidelity and mechanical requirements would support a dedicated engineering-design track. Mixing their scores into a visual-communication index would change what that index measures. [Current tasks and tool regimes](https://benchcad.com/) |
| StarVector, ASCII Tree, Penrose Diagrams | Converting a supplied raster image to SVG tests tracing fidelity. Generating a prescribed tree or a compilable TikZ diagram tests structured representation. None of the reviewed environment records established a common, attributable five-provider model panel grading original visual communication. Preserve diagram composition as a coverage gap. [StarVector environment](https://app.primeintellect.ai/dashboard/environments/ob1/starvector) · [ASCII Tree](https://app.primeintellect.ai/dashboard/environments/primeintellect/ascii-tree) · [Penrose Diagrams](https://app.primeintellect.ai/dashboard/environments/primeintellect/penrose-diagrams) |
| Graphic-Design-Bench | This producer offers promising isolated typography, layout and editing tasks. Its published model groups distinguish multimodal LLMs, image generators and video generators. The documented multimodal comparisons name OpenAI, Google and Anthropic, short of five eligible providers. A whole-suite score would also repeat vector and animation work. Revisit a single typography or design-editing task when more model configurations and comparable scores become available. [Code and data](https://github.com/purvanshi/lica-bench) · [Producer results](https://lica.world/blog/gdb-real-world-benchmark-for-graphic-design) |
| BizGenEval | Commercial briefs for ads, posters and slides are directly relevant to designers. The producer evaluates **image-generation systems** on their rendered images. Their scores and generation costs cannot be assigned to separately named language-model configurations. [Microsoft’s evaluation suite](https://github.com/microsoft/BizGenEval) |
| Chart Extraction, DocVQA and VisualWebArena | These require extracting values, answering questions about an existing document or operating an existing site. Their outputs do not grade a designed artifact. [Chart Extraction](https://sophon.at/evals/prime-13point5-chart-extraction) · [DocVQA](https://www.docvqa.org/) · [VisualWebArena](https://github.com/web-arena-x/visualwebarena) |

The audit checks overlap in the work requested, source splits and reused scores. Private prompts, including Hemingway’s full panel and private website submissions, prevent a complete item-by-item overlap check. A fresh rubric, another publisher or a separate leaderboard URL cannot by itself establish a distinct task.
