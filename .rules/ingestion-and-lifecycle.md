# Ingestion pipeline and lifecycle automation

## Mandatory adapter contract

- Every adapter in `MANDATORY_ADAPTERS` (`scripts/ingest/run.ts`, 17 mandatory sources: `artificial-analysis`, `aikido`, `cwe-bench`, `cyber-arena`, `epoch`, `frontier-harness`, `lmarena`, `tbench`, `harness-leaderboard`, `livebench`, `vals`, `vulcanbench`, `designarena`, `writing-feeds`, `scale-seal`, `surge`, `pricing`) is mandatory and uses relative imports (`../../src/...`) for Bun 1.4.x and Node.js 26 LTS parity.
- `scripts/ingest/sources/frontier-harness.ts` fetches FrontierHarness Eval v1.1 (`https://frontierharness.org/api/live?since=0` + `/api/summary`, 22 harnesses × 5 open control models × 62 tasks × 4 attempts = 27,280 runs), enforces distinct non-empty `(subset, taskName)` identities, unique `finished_fields` column names, unique `live.outcomes` codes, hard minimum subset task floors (`tb21 >= 45`, `tb4 >= 17`), explicit integer `attempts >= 2`, and raw numeric `0 | 1` `lookup` values on every row, deduplicates identical replay variants before counting unique conflicts so replays never cross rejection thresholds, marks conflicting duplicates corrupted to reject affected slices, requires complete `(model, task)` cell coverage (`cellMap.size >= totalExpectedTasks * modelCountInSlice`) and `>= 90%` run completion per slice, excludes `"infra"` outcomes, scores reward-hacking `lookup > 0` passes as failures, averages non-negative priced attempts (`parseNonNegative`) within each `(model, task)` cell first before equal-task averaging (`costCount > 0` per cell and `subCostMean > 0`), and emits per-benchmark and overall `passRate`, `taskClusteredCiPct` (`ciIsUpperBound: false` when measured), and per-benchmark-tracked exact unbiased `pass^3`.
- `scripts/ingest/sources/harness-leaderboard.ts` fetches the Apache-2.0 Coding-Agent Harness Leaderboard (`https://joelniklaus-harness-leaderboard.hf.space/`, inline `#hlb-data` JSON), validates contributing single-effort slices at full source precision (integer `n_scored >= authN`, exact asymmetric single-effort `[ciLower, ciUpper]`, separate lower/upper delta propagation on averages), requires boolean `complete === true`, unrounded `macro` agreeing within `0.15` pp of validated constituents and rollup rates agreeing within `0.15` pp of validated contributors, authoritative `config.benchmarks[bmId].subset_sizes.full`, exact `n_tasks === n_tasks_expected === effortMultiplier * authUniqueN`, reconciled `row.n_scored === sum(bm.n_scored)`, `>= 8` complete harnesses per slice, row+constituent `n_tasks3` and Fréchet-Hoeffding `p3 >= max(0, 3p - 200) - 0.15` feasibility before publishing `passAll3Rate` (clearing constituent `passAll3Rate` when row eligibility fails), and explicit `ciIsUpperBound: boolean` on every benchmark. `compileDomainsFromSources` (`scripts/ingest/run.ts`) merges both into one unified `harnessLeaderboard` via `mergeControlledHarnessSuites`, and BOTH live `refreshMandatorySource` and `readSourceCacheStrict` (`scripts/ingest/cache.ts`) enforce exact source-specific benchmark rosters via `validateHarnessSourceBenchmarks`.
- `scripts/ingest/sources/cwe-bench.ts` ingests the registered 250-task (`100` public + `150` held-out exact split, every row `tasks === 250`) CWE-bench v2 dataset (`data-v2.js` + required nonempty unique HTML table `tds[1]` revision-qualified model names & harness, stable chart-key `sourceRowId` for caching & disqualification, `ci95`, measured cost, `<time datetime>` / `dateModified` publication date, and unambiguous v2-only protocol effort marking the partition degraded if missing) and `cyber-arena.ts` ingests CyberArena Attack-Defense CTF (paired with single-scaffold `aikido.ts`); `surge.ts` parses the balanced `<div role="list">` containing `data-leaderboard-row=""` (requiring a matched closing tag) across 7 Surge AI leaderboards (`surge_sudo_l7`, `surge_complex_constraints`, `surge_gdp_xlsx`, `surge_dayjob_finance`, `surge_dayjob_law`, `surge_dayjob_healthcare`, `surge_riemann`), validates CI containment across both Elo (`<= 30`) and percent feeds, maps `(No reasoning)` to `"none"`, and quarantines `(Auto reasoning)`, `(default)`, and `(Adaptive/Default/...)`; `epoch.ts` reads 6 unsaturated disjoint CSVs from `benchmark_data.zip` via built-in `node:zlib` PKZIP extraction (`epoch_mirrorcode`, `epoch_ale_bench`, `epoch_frontierswe`, `epoch_cursorbench`, `epoch_simpleqa_verified`, `epoch_apex_agents`), verifies every declared required column (including `costCol` and `dateCol`) exists in `parsed.meta.fields`, reconciles `Name` against `Model version` (rejecting conflicting model identities or efforts), strips inference-host suffixes on either side of effort suffixes, checks `isRegisteredModelExact` before effort stripping, overrides strictly `_unknown` with verified effort column while quarantining effort contradictions, and includes resolved effort in ID-less row keys; `lmarena.ts` keeps 1 non-overlapping split per active crowd arena (`lmarena_creative_writing`, `lmarena_webdev`, `lmarena_factuality`, CI `<= 25`) plus `lmarena_agent` (`agent_capability:code`, `>= 300` sessions, CI `<= 60` on the 135-SD capability scale) in Coding, requiring explicit published `ciLower`/`ciUpper` bounds containing the rating within `0.01`; `designarena.ts` requires `json.success === true` across 4 disjoint arenas (`designarena_website`, `designarena_svg`, `designarena_3d`, `designarena_gamedev`), requiring `>= 350` battles when `btStdErr` is unreported and strictly validating `btStdErr` and integer counts (`parseStrictNonNegativeInt`); `writing-feeds.ts` ingests `lechmazur_writing` (with CI containment and exact duplicate checks), `eqbench_creative_writing` (0-20 rubric score, `sampleSize: 32`), and `eqbench_longform_writing` (0-100 multi-chapter rubric score, `sampleSize: 12`) with stable `sourceRowId`s; `scale-seal.ts` ingests `scale_prbench_finance` and `scale_prbench_legal`, stripping `one-pass-agent` tokens inside standalone or compound parentheticals and keeping `seRaw: null` for `"upstream_ci"` provenance; `livebench.ts` keeps unsaturated `livebench_long_agentic` (`sampleSize: 18`); `vals.ts` fetches each benchmark's `BenchmarkViewLoader` `benchmarkViewUrl` JSON as the primary `benchmarkView` payload, reconciles `reasoning_effort` and `compute_effort` across external JSON and inline Astro islands, and enforces a per-benchmark effort-recovery circuit breaker; adapters compare every normalized `RawObservation` field strictly (`observationsMatchExact`) order-independently and emit conflicting `sourceRowId`s in `disqualifiedSourceRowIdsByBenchmark` so degraded cache retention never restores quarantined rows. `writing-feeds.ts` attributes verified August 20 `DeepSeek V4 Pro (high)` to `deepseek-v4-pro-0813` while preserving `evalDate: null` and parsing latest changelog `publishedDate`. External-harness overrides (`rawHarness !== null`) are excluded on all non-Harnesses domains inside `extractAcceptedCellsFromSources`, and BOTH live `refreshMandatorySource` and `readSourceCacheStrict` enforce strict `sourceId === adapter.id` ownership per cache file.
- `scripts/ingest/cache.ts` loads existing caches strictly via `readSourceCacheStrict`, merges observation partitions, pricing, and `harnessLeaderboard` uniformly through rejection (`> 10%`), 3-day `.shrink-state.json` shrink confirmation (`__harness_leaderboard__` sub-feed + row/slice drop detection), and upstream timestamp rollback/staleness gates (`compareIsoInstants < 0` or `> 14d` old retains healthy cache as degraded), strips `disqualifiedSourceRowIdsByBenchmark` from degraded retained partitions, and excludes live `conflictingPricingApiSkus`/`conflictingPricingModelSlugs` from cached price fallback.
- Validate upstream versions, required columns, and per-row metadata joins inside adapters before replacing healthy partitions: `vals.ts` requires `typeof metadata.version === "string"`, unconditionally parses `BenchmarkViewLoader` + `refusal_fallback` (both `JSON.parse` and Rolldown inline object literals without `eval`), excludes gated `-range`/`daybreak` variants, rejects contradictory effort labels, and preserves `seRaw`; `tbench.ts` emits `tbench_4_1` into domain `"harnesses"`, enforces `MIN_TBENCH_4_1_TRIALS = 330` (66 tasks x 5 repeats), requires explicit non-null `reasoning_effort`, rejects malformed metadata and token-count conflicts, and divides run token totals by `n_trials`; `livebench.ts` validates `cost.csv` headers and divides category token totals by `nq_*`; `artificial-analysis.ts` parses `/leaderboards/models`, `/evaluations/aa-briefcase`, `/evaluations/gdpval-aa`, `/evaluations/code2video-aa`, and `/agents/coding-agents` into unified identity records with strict effort provenance; `scale-seal.ts` extracts exact `confidenceInterval_upper` half-widths; `http.ts` retries transient 5xx/timeout errors once cleanly.
- Concatenate Next.js Flight chunks verbatim via an O(n) linear scanner and parse dates strictly in UTC via `parseIsoDate` (`scripts/ingest/http.ts`). Use `fetchArrayBufferWithSafeAuth` for credential-safe multi-hop HTTPS redirects.

## Immutable epochs, cryptographic manifest, and full-recompute validation

- `scripts/ingest/lock.ts` holds an OS-kernel cross-process publish lock via `node:sqlite` (`DatabaseSync` `BEGIN EXCLUSIVE` on `data/epochs/.publish.lock.db` with `busy_timeout = 0` and `journal_mode = DELETE`, identical across Bun 1.4.x and Node.js 26 LTS) plus `AsyncLocalStorage` re-entrancy.
- `scripts/ingest/generation.ts`, `scripts/ingest/cache.ts`, `scripts/ingest/sync.ts`, and `src/lib/data.ts` implement WAL-journaled generation staging, `"r+"` `fsyncFile`, POSIX `fsyncDir`, idempotent copy-replace rollback, and cold-load coalescing.
- `scripts/ingest/run.ts` recomputes `harnessLeaderboard` independently via `recomputeHarnessLeaderboard` + `HarnessLeaderboardSnapshotSchema.parse` inside `compileDomainsFromSources` (never blindly copying cached frontier annotations), validates historical MAJOR epoch archives via `validateFrozenMajorEpochSnapshot` (checking archived `rm.coverageFloor`, complete `(regime, provider, harnessFilter)` roster across valid regimes up-front, deterministic frontier/pruned metrics, `::all` `paretoProb`/`imputationSensitive` config reconciliation, slice-specific `imputationSensitive` and non-imputed `paretoProb`, `verdicts`, and joint-link Woodbury REML parity), refuses to trust unpinned on-disk MAJOR archives, refuses to freeze a new MAJOR regime whose active benchmarks all already hit hard lifecycle triggers, preflights all pins/chains/digests before any `fs.link`, and pins SHA-256 hashes in `data/epochs/manifest.json`.
- `scripts/ingest/audit-lifecycle.ts` keys prior audits by `benchmarkId::harnessRegime::benchmarkVersion`, recovers WALs, validates `lifecycleOverride` before mutating disk, and compares instants via nanosecond `compareIsoInstants`.
- `scripts/ingest/validate-snapshot.ts` enforces strict `HarnessLeaderboardSnapshotSchema` (`slices.length >= 1`, `slices[0].sliceId === "_all"`, unique IDs, CI bounds, `passAll3Rate <= passRate`, valid `dominatedById`, upstream timestamp freshness), zero-overlap `datasetLineageId` and unique LMArena split validation per domain (including `lmarena_agent`), Popoviciu `sampleSize` agreement against uniform non-arena upstream task counts, v2+ required frozen anchor `redundancyFactor`/`taskFamily`/`datasetLineageId` plus algebraic `effectiveWeight` product parity and live registry match, independent `recomputeHarnessLeaderboard` parity, `regimeMeta.costSource`/`measuredCostBenchmarkIds`/`priorMean`/`tauModelSq` parity, weighted-zero Rasch delta gauge (`|sum(w*delta)| <= 1e-3`), verified API pricing and `infoReliability >= 0.50` on every eligible config, strict fail-closed `computeConfigPosterior` (`shrinkageFactor`, `theta`, `se`, `samplingSe`, `residualSe`) verification, manifest-pinned archive validation (including all-config REML `priorMean`/`tauModelSq` recomputation), full deterministic raw-to-release recompute parity, and `data/lifecycle.prev.json` verification.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` sets `STRICT_FRESHNESS: "1"`, validates multi-commit batch pushes step-by-step using each commit's own `git archive "$COMMIT"` tree, and enforces append-only `data/epochs/manifest.json` and nanosecond chronology.
