# Ingestion pipeline and lifecycle automation

## Mandatory adapter contract

- Every adapter in `MANDATORY_ADAPTERS` (`scripts/ingest/run.ts`, 15 mandatory sources: `artificial-analysis`, `aikido`, `arcprize`, `cwe-bench`, `cyber-arena`, `epoch`, `frontier-harness`, `lmarena`, `harness-leaderboard`, `livebench`, `vals`, `designarena`, `surge`, `tonebench`, `pricing`) is mandatory and uses relative imports (`../../src/...`) for Bun 1.4.x and Node.js 26 LTS parity.
- `scripts/ingest/sources/frontier-harness.ts` fetches FrontierHarness Eval v1.1 (`https://frontierharness.org/api/live?since=0` + `/api/summary`, 22 harnesses × 5 open control models × 62 tasks × 4 attempts = 27,280 runs), enforces distinct non-empty `(subset, taskName)` identities, unique `finished_fields` column names, unique `live.outcomes` codes, hard minimum subset task floors (`tb21 >= 45`, `tb4 >= 17`), explicit integer `attempts >= 2`, and raw numeric `0 | 1` `lookup` values on every row, deduplicates identical replay variants before counting unique conflicts so replays never cross rejection thresholds, marks conflicting duplicates corrupted to reject affected slices, requires complete `(model, task)` cell coverage (`cellMap.size >= totalExpectedTasks * modelCountInSlice`) and `>= 90%` run completion per slice, excludes `"infra"` outcomes, scores reward-hacking `lookup > 0` passes as failures, averages non-negative priced attempts (`parseNonNegative`) within each `(model, task)` cell first before equal-task averaging (`costCount > 0` per cell and `subCostMean > 0`), and emits per-benchmark and overall `passRate`, `taskClusteredCiPct` (`ciIsUpperBound: false` when measured), and per-benchmark-tracked exact unbiased `pass^3`.
- `scripts/ingest/sources/harness-leaderboard.ts` fetches the Apache-2.0 Coding-Agent Harness Leaderboard (`https://joelniklaus-harness-leaderboard.hf.space/`, inline `#hlb-data` JSON), validates contributing single-effort slices at full source precision (integer `n_scored >= authN`, exact asymmetric single-effort `[ciLower, ciUpper]`, separate lower/upper delta propagation on averages), requires boolean `complete === true`, unrounded `macro` agreeing within `0.15` pp of validated constituents and rollup rates agreeing within `0.15` pp of validated contributors, authoritative `config.benchmarks[bmId].subset_sizes.full`, exact `n_tasks === n_tasks_expected === effortMultiplier * authUniqueN`, reconciled `row.n_scored === sum(bm.n_scored)`, `>= 8` complete harnesses per slice, row+constituent `n_tasks3` and Fréchet-Hoeffding `p3 >= max(0, 3p - 200) - 0.15` feasibility before publishing `passAll3Rate` (clearing constituent `passAll3Rate` when row eligibility fails), and explicit `ciIsUpperBound: boolean` on every benchmark. `compileDomainsFromSources` (`scripts/ingest/run.ts`) merges both into one unified `harnessLeaderboard` via `mergeControlledHarnessSuites`, and BOTH live `refreshMandatorySource` and `readSourceCacheStrict` (`scripts/ingest/cache.ts`) enforce exact source-specific benchmark rosters via `validateHarnessSourceBenchmarks`.
- `scripts/ingest/sources/cwe-bench.ts` ingests strictly the 150-task closed held-out split (`benchmarkVersion: "2.0-heldout"`, `sampleSize: 150`, `battleCount: 600` across 4 repeats) via `data-p1-heldout` and `data-cost-heldout` attributes on `cwe-bench.com` HTML rows (joined against `data-v2.js` `cost.points` enforcing `nTasks === 100`, `nPrivate === 150`, `pt.tasks === 250`, and weighted public+heldout split identity within `0.15` pp, stable chart-key `sourceRowId`, revision-qualified model/harness validation, `<time datetime>` / `dateModified` publication date, and unambiguous v2-only protocol effort marking the partition degraded if missing) and `cyber-arena.ts` ingests live CyberArena Attack-Defense CTF (paired with single-scaffold private `aikido.ts`); `surge.ts` parses the balanced `<div role="list">` containing `data-leaderboard-row=""` (requiring a matched closing tag) across 11 strictly private Surge AI leaderboards (`surge_sudo_l7`, `surge_gdp_xlsx`, `surge_dayjob_finance`, `surge_dayjob_law`, `surge_dayjob_healthcare`, `surge_corecraft`, `surge_riemann`, `surge_dayjob_tax`, `surge_handbook`, `surge_chartography`, `surge_complex_constraints`), validates CI containment across both Elo (`<= 30`) and percent feeds, maps `(No reasoning)` to `"none"`, and quarantines `(Auto reasoning)`, `(default)`, and `(Adaptive/Default/...)`; `epoch.ts` reads strictly private `epoch_cursorbench` and `epoch_frontiermath_t13` (`sampleSize: 285` private set, rejecting malformed negative/non-numeric `stderr`) from `benchmark_data.zip` via built-in `node:zlib` PKZIP extraction, verifies every declared required column exists in `parsed.meta.fields`, reconciles `Name` against `Model version` (rejecting conflicting model identities or efforts), strips inference-host suffixes on either side of effort suffixes, checks `isRegisteredModelExact` before effort stripping, overrides strictly `_unknown` with verified effort column while quarantining effort contradictions, and includes resolved effort in ID-less row keys; `lmarena.ts` keeps 1 non-overlapping live blind-arena split per active arena (`lmarena_creative_writing`, `lmarena_webdev`, `lmarena_factuality`, CI `<= 25`) plus `lmarena_agent` (`agent_capability:code`, Coding) and `lmarena_agent_work` (`agent_capability:work`, Research) (`>= 300` sessions, CI `<= 60` on the 135-SD capability scale), requiring explicit published `ciLower`/`ciUpper` bounds containing the rating within `0.01`; `designarena.ts` requires `json.success === true` across 7 disjoint live arenas (5 single-shot `models` arenas `website`, `svg`, `3d`, `gamedev`, `dataviz` + 2 agentic `agents` arenas `mobileapps`, `agon_slides`), requiring `>= 350` battles when `btStdErr` is unreported and strictly validating `btStdErr` and integer counts (`parseStrictNonNegativeInt`); - `scripts/ingest/cache.ts` loads existing caches strictly via `readSourceCacheStrict`, merges observation partitions, pricing, and `harnessLeaderboard` uniformly through rejection (`> 10%`), 3-day `.shrink-state.json` shrink confirmation (`__harness_leaderboard__` sub-feed + row/slice drop detection), and upstream timestamp rollback guards (`compareIsoInstants < 0` retains healthy cache as degraded; completed static studies never fail solely on age), strips `disqualifiedSourceRowIdsByBenchmark` from degraded retained partitions, and excludes live `conflictingPricingApiSkus`/`conflictingPricingModelSlugs` from cached price fallback.

- `scripts/ingest/validate-snapshot.ts` anchors `derivedStaleDays` to `snapshot.generatedAt` (with live `MAX_SNAPSHOT_AGE_HOURS=24` in `daily-ingest.yml`), enforces strict `<= 7d` freshness on `pricing`, and applies quorum + per-domain liveness (`<= 2` long-stale non-pricing sources and `>= 1` fresh source per domain + controlled harness board) so 1 to 2 dead third-party sites never freeze healthy domains.
- `.github/workflows/daily-ingest.yml` runs `bun run update:readme` (`scripts/ingest/update-readme-assets.ts`) to keep `README.md` and `docs/assets/index-matrix-{light,dark}.svg` live, adds a 45-day keepalive commit guard against GitHub's 60-day cron auto-disable, retries transient `git push` transport errors without bypassing non-fast-forward guards, and runs `scripts/ingest/health-alert.ts` (`if: always()`) to maintain one auto-closing `automation-health` GitHub issue.
- Validate upstream versions, required columns, and per-row metadata joins inside adapters before replacing healthy partitions: `vals.ts` requires `typeof metadata.version === "string"`, unconditionally parses `BenchmarkViewLoader` + `refusal_fallback` (both `JSON.parse` and Rolldown inline object literals without `eval`), excludes gated `-range`/`daybreak` variants, rejects contradictory effort labels, persists exact `refusals / totalTasks` (fallback `refusalRatePct`) when positive, and preserves `seRaw`; `livebench.ts` validates `cost.csv` headers and divides category token totals by `nq_*`; `artificial-analysis.ts` parses `/leaderboards/models`, `/evaluations/aa-briefcase`, and `/agents/coding-agents` across 8 strictly private benchmarks (including `aa_harvey_lab` and `aa_agent_frontiercode` emitting `suiteCostUsd` strictly when `evalCount === 3 && indexComponentCount === 3`) into unified identity records with strict effort provenance; `http.ts` retries transient 5xx/timeout errors cleanly.
- Concatenate Next.js Flight chunks verbatim via an O(n) linear scanner (`extractRscText`), decode UTF-8 byte-framed `T`-chunks and resolve `$undefined`/`$$`/lowercase-hex `$<chunkId>:<path>` outline references via `parseRscFlightChunks` + `resolveRscFlightTree`, and parse dates strictly in UTC via `parseIsoDate` (`scripts/ingest/http.ts`). ## Immutable epochs, cryptographic manifest, and full-recompute validation

- `scripts/ingest/lock.ts` holds an OS-kernel cross-process publish lock via `node:sqlite` (`DatabaseSync` `BEGIN EXCLUSIVE` on `data/epochs/.publish.lock.db` with `busy_timeout = 0` and `journal_mode = DELETE`, identical across Bun 1.4.x and Node.js 26 LTS) plus `AsyncLocalStorage` re-entrancy.
- `scripts/ingest/generation.ts`, `scripts/ingest/cache.ts`, `scripts/ingest/sync.ts`, and `src/lib/data.ts` implement WAL-journaled generation staging, `"r+"` `fsyncFile`, POSIX `fsyncDir`, idempotent copy-replace rollback, and cold-load coalescing.
- `scripts/ingest/run.ts` recomputes `harnessLeaderboard` independently via `recomputeHarnessLeaderboard` + `HarnessLeaderboardSnapshotSchema.parse` inside `compileDomainsFromSources` (never blindly copying cached frontier annotations), validates historical MAJOR epoch archives via `validateFrozenMajorEpochSnapshot` (checking archived `rm.coverageFloor`, complete `(regime, provider, harnessFilter)` roster across valid regimes up-front, deterministic frontier/pruned metrics, `::all` `paretoProb`/`imputationSensitive` config reconciliation, slice-specific `imputationSensitive` and non-imputed `paretoProb`, `verdicts`, and joint-link Woodbury REML parity), refuses to trust unpinned on-disk MAJOR archives, refuses to freeze a new MAJOR regime whose active benchmarks all already hit hard lifecycle triggers, preflights all pins/chains/digests before any `fs.link`, and pins SHA-256 hashes in `data/epochs/manifest.json`.
- `scripts/ingest/audit-lifecycle.ts` keys prior audits by `benchmarkId::harnessRegime::benchmarkVersion`, recovers WALs, validates `lifecycleOverride` before mutating disk, and compares instants via nanosecond `compareIsoInstants`.
- `scripts/ingest/validate-snapshot.ts` enforces strict `HarnessLeaderboardSnapshotSchema` (`slices.length >= 1`, `slices[0].sliceId === "_all"`, unique IDs, CI bounds, `passAll3Rate <= passRate`, valid `dominatedById`, upstream timestamp freshness), zero-overlap `datasetLineageId` and unique LMArena split validation per domain (including `lmarena_agent`), Popoviciu `sampleSize` agreement against uniform non-arena upstream task counts, v2+ required frozen anchor `redundancyFactor`/`taskFamily`/`datasetLineageId` plus algebraic `effectiveWeight` product parity and live registry match, independent `recomputeHarnessLeaderboard` parity, `regimeMeta.costSource`/`measuredCostBenchmarkIds`/`priorMean`/`tauModelSq` parity, weighted-zero Rasch delta gauge (`|sum(w*delta)| <= 1e-3`), verified API pricing and `infoReliability >= 0.50` on every eligible config, strict fail-closed `computeConfigPosterior` (`shrinkageFactor`, `theta`, `se`, `samplingSe`, `residualSe`) verification, manifest-pinned archive validation (including all-config REML `priorMean`/`tauModelSq` recomputation), full deterministic raw-to-release recompute parity, and `data/lifecycle.prev.json` verification.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` sets `STRICT_FRESHNESS: "1"`, validates multi-commit batch pushes step-by-step using each commit's own `git archive "$COMMIT"` tree, and enforces append-only `data/epochs/manifest.json` and nanosecond chronology.
