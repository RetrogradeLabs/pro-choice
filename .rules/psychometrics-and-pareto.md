## Reliability-discrimination weighted Rasch invariants (`src/lib/math/aggregate.ts`)

Preserve `measurementFamily`, `datasetPools`, `weightPolicy`, `qualityWeight` and `effectiveWeight` in the frozen anchor. Registry changes require a MAJOR epoch. - Expose decomposed `samplingSe` and `residualSe` on every `EvaluatedConfig`, persist `seRaw`, `fallbackRouting`, and `refusalRate` on `BenchmarkScoreDetail` and `usesFallbackRouting` on `EvaluatedConfig`, and resolve `suiteCostUsd` strictly from the freshest healthy `subFeedHealthyAt` cells. - Standardize broad benchmarks with lab-balanced weights (`w_i = 1 / K_lab(i)`), freezing `introducedAt` and `invertScore` on every `AnchorStats` record so historical archive replays never depend on mutable registry definitions. Link sparse (`< minLabs`) and range-restricted elite-subset benchmarks (distinct model breadth `maxAnchorModels >= 35 && anchorModelCount < maxAnchorModels * 0.4`, e.g. `aa_briefcase`) via iterative lab-balanced Kolen and Brennan `linkBenchmarkMeanSigma` and build `RegimeMeta.linkJointCov` via true joint delete-one jackknife over the UNION of all configs in all linked panels (full IEEE-754 precision Gram matrix synchronized with `AnchorStats` 2×2 blocks and validated symmetric PSD in `LinkJointCovSchema`); ALWAYS withhold if Mean-Sigma linking fails.
- `solveTwoWayLinearRasch` uses exact weighted Schur-complement normal equations with weighted rank-1 gauge constraint `sum(w_b * delta_b) = 0`, computes analytical Gauss-Markov `thetaVarByConfig` and scale-invariant constrained-subspace `deltaCovByBenchmark` (`tauResidSq * M^-1 * (A * A^T) * M^-1`), exports `weightedDf` and `tauResidIdentified`, debiases weighted-projection `tauResidSq` strictly using genuine upstream sampling variance (`upstream_ci` or `upstream_se` via wide-normal-stable `clippedNormalVariance`, applied identically in calibration, live posterior, and archive `computeConfigPosterior`, with optional `debiasVarZ` opt-out so Popoviciu upper bounds, `UNREPORTED_SE_Z`, averaged-effort/model CI upper bounds, or shared Mean-Sigma `linkVarZ` are NEVER subtracted), Winsorizes benchmark z-scores (`clampZScore` in `[-3.0, +3.5]`), and keeps only components bridged by frozen anchors or the single largest connected component. - Fit `(priorMean, tauModelSq)` via global-maximum block-correlated heteroscedastic REML (`fitHeteroscedasticRemlPrior` using exact provider-block Cholesky with the same `rho=0.5` family / `rho=0.1` provider residual covariance as ranking plus exact Matrix Determinant Lemma + Woodbury low-rank cross-provider joint Mean-Sigma link covariance via `linkJointCov` / `RemlLinkTerm`, 120-point log grid with golden-section refinement on EVERY local peak and endpoint on `[0.10, 2.50]`, frozen in `RegimeMeta` per MAJOR epoch) and apply Morris Empirical Bayes shrinkage (`B_i = tauModelSq / (tauModelSq + v_i)`, `theta = priorMean + B_i * (thetaMle - priorMean)`, `se = 15 * sqrt(B_i * v_i)`, `shrinkageFactor = B_i`, `score = 50 + 15 * theta`). Require `shrinkageFactor >= MIN_FRONTIER_SHRINKAGE` (`0.50`) so at least half of every frontier score comes from observed benchmark evidence without arbitrary `"Under N benchmarks"` cliffs. `computeConfigPosterior` strictly requires frozen `popoviciuSe` on `popoviciu_upper_bound` cells, fails closed (`exactSeRecomputable = false`) on missing SE metadata, and is the single shared verifier across `run.ts`, `validate-snapshot.ts`, and `lifecycle.ts`.

## Saturation lifecycle math (`src/lib/math/lifecycle.ts`)

- `computeRankEvidence` in `src/lib/math/rank-audit.ts` compares eligible pairs on their shared frozen, weighted benchmark scores. For each held-out feed, exclude all its cells and columns before refitting the epoch's aggregation method. Score the equal-weight baseline on the identical matched configurations and publish the denominator with Kendall tau-b.
- At calibration, require SE coverage across at least half the provider-balanced observations for the empirical reliability estimate. Each provider contributes its fraction of scored rows with usable SE. Frozen epochs retain their published weights.
- `computeSourceSensitivity` removes all active benchmarks on one ingestion feed at a time. Keep score calibration and costs fixed. Among eligible configs that used the feed, report the count retaining enough coverage and precision and the Kendall tau-b of their published score order before and after removal.

- Evaluate every probation and retirement gate on unrounded full-precision quantities. Parse timestamps strictly in UTC via `parseStrictUtcDateMs`. Persist `benchmarkVersion` on every `BenchmarkAudit` and reset retirement streaks when a benchmark's version changes.
