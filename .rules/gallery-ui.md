# Gallery UI, visual identity, and frontend optimization

## Aesthetic system (Retrograde Labs warm archival taste, zero AI slop)

- ProChoice is a product of Retrograde Labs (`https://inretrograde.io`): warm archival light canvas (`#fafaf8`, ink `#111111`, elevated `#ffffff`, subtle `#f5f4f0`) and warm espresso dark canvas (`#1a1816`, ink `#f5f4f0`, surface `#211f1c`, subtle `#262320`), defaulting to the OS system theme (`prefers-color-scheme`) with single-click toggle to the opposite theme (clearing `localStorage` override when toggling back to match system).
- Left sticky navigation rail with clickable optically-centered rounded-border Choice Fork `BrandMark` (`src/components/ProChoiceGallery.tsx`, matching `src/app/icon.svg` and `src/app/page.tsx`) + `prochoice` wordmark only (no slogan line under the logo; `lg:pt-24` aligned cleanly with Home and domain page top padding), vertical domain links (`Coding`, `Writing`, `Design`, `Research`, `Cyber`, `Harnesses`), no `"Methodology"` item in the sidebar or mobile header, and an icon-only dark/light toggle button vertically centered in a `66px` bottom slot aligned pixel-for-pixel with the `67px` footer text (`Updated YYYY-MM-DD`, `© YYYY Retrograde Labs`); central hairline-framed content column, clean grotesk sans (`Inter`), editorial upright serif headings (`Newsreader` 400/500, no italic preload), and tabular monospace figures (`JetBrains Mono`). Architectural loading skeletons (`GalleryShellFallback` in `src/app/page.tsx` and inline domain loader in `ProChoiceGallery.tsx`) mirror the rounded Choice Fork rail, elevated white plates, and Pareto trace.
- Zero AI slop and zero decorative chrome: no `.blueprint-grid` chart wallpaper, no decorative eyebrow stat bars (`"9 labs · 6 domains · 12 harnesses"`), no `"01 / 02 / 03"` marketing cards, no `"Benchmarks"` (`3/4`) table column, no boxy `border-l-2` badge cards, no `>= 1 SE gain` / `< 1 SE tie` chart legends, no dashed frontier lines, and no jargon badges (`"Missing-data sensitive"`, `"Fallback"`, `"±SE"`, `"Imputed"`, `"Est"`) cluttering main leaderboard surfaces.
- Use consistent, intuitive terminology everywhere: **`Provider`** (never `"Lab"` or `"Maker"`), **`<Domain> Composite Index`** for every domain title (Home plates, page `<h1>`, and Methodology drawer title), **`Input / output ($/1M)`** (never `"List $/1M"`), **`Cost / task`** on model domains, **`Cost / run`**, **`Cost / solved`**, **`Pass all 3`**, **`Harnesses only`**, **`Harness + model`**, **`Test model`**, and a per-instrument **`All N models`** on Harnesses (with Harnesses ALWAYS leading with harness names on cards, table rows, and hover pills on BOTH tabs, while ALL OTHER 5 domains MUST NEVER display any harness label or `observedHarness` anywhere), and descriptive controlled tags (`Lowest cost`, `Cost–score tradeoff`, `Highest pass rate`). Never use unexplained jargon like `"Dominated options"` in first-visit hero copy.
- Design tokens, `--selection-bg`/`--selection-fg`, `.thin-scrollbar` thumb, and distinct provider inks (`--prov-*` for all 9 providers across light, dark, and `prefers-color-scheme: dark`) live exclusively in `src/app/globals.css`. Pair every provider ink with a distinct geometric `ProviderMarkerIcon` (`src/components/ProviderMarkerIcon.tsx`, including 4-point `"star"` for Meta) so color is never the sole differentiator (WCAG 1.4.1).
- Wrap `{children}` in `src/app/layout.tsx` inside `<div className="isolate">` with `relative` on `<body>`. Website title is `"ProChoice | No Nonsense LLM Leaderboards & Rankings"`.

## Home page, slim payload, and domain views

- `src/app/page.tsx` and `src/app/[domain]/page.tsx` statically prerender `/` and all 6 canonical domain routes (`/coding`, `/writing`, `/design`, `/research`, `/cyber`, `/harnesses`) at build time via `loadCachedRelease()` (`"use cache"` + `cacheLife("max")` in `src/lib/release-cache.ts`), injecting domain-specific metadata, dynamic `opengraph-image.tsx` cards, and Schema.org JSON-LD (`WebSite`, `DataCatalog`, `Dataset`, `BreadcrumbList`, `ItemList`) from `src/lib/seo.ts`. `ProChoiceGallery.tsx` and `HomeView.tsx` render real `<Link href>` anchors for crawlers while keeping instant directional client transitions via `history.pushState` and atomic, on-demand `/api/snapshot` hydration when navigation or methodology needs more data. Compare all domain epochs before adopting the response. `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, `/api/markdown` (via `Accept: text/markdown` and `/*.md` edge rewrites in `vercel.ts`), `/openapi.json`, `/.well-known/api-catalog`, `/.well-known/security.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, and `/robots.txt` provide full search and AI-agent discovery.
- Bare `/` renders `HomeView.tsx` with generous Lune-inspired editorial hero top padding (`lg:pt-24 lg:pb-10`), a single-line Title Case desktop `<h1>` `"No Nonsense LLM Leaderboards & Rankings"`, plain first-visit description (`max-w-[960px] text-pretty`, emphasizing that models costing more while scoring lower are left off the chart, zero jargon), an immediate 2×3 hairline-bordered interactive domain ledger aligned to the exact `lg:px-11` gutter (`<article>` cards with accessible `<h2><button>` triggers, real Pareto sparklines, and live `Top score` + `Best value` / `Budget` picks synced to `extractVerdictPicks`), and a bottom-pinned colophon (`Updated YYYY-MM-DD`, `© YYYY Retrograde Labs`). Domain/Home/tab navigation uses `history.pushState` with spatially directional motion: desktop vertical sidebar animates top-to-bottom / bottom-to-top, while mobile horizontal nav bar (`< 1024px`) and Harnesses tab / Test model transitions animate left-to-right / right-to-left while keeping the heading and tab bar stationary (0px shift).
- Default provider selection (`src/lib/gallery-url.ts`) is all 5 Western providers (`anthropic`, `openai`, `google`, `spacexai`, `meta`), preserved across Harnesses visits. `ProviderPicker.tsx` renders all 9 providers on a non-wrapping single-row toggle strip (`no-scrollbar overflow-x-auto sm:flex-wrap`, `aria-pressed`, double-click or `Shift+Enter` to solo, zero parenthetical titles, no `"Western/Chinese"` buttons), `"All 9"` / `"Reset"`, and a flat `"Pruned"` toggle (plus `aria-pressed` harness filters on `Model + harness`).
- `VerdictPlacards.tsx` pools all eligible priced configs from selected providers in one pass (`computeProviderFrontier`) and selects cards strictly via `extractVerdictPicks(..., { preferDistinctModelSlug: !isSingleLabSelection })` into a hairline-divided architectural typographic strip aligned to `lg:px-11` (`Budget`, `Best value` with 2px `var(--interactive)` top accent bar when statistically supported, `Top score`).
- `HarnessesView.tsx` shows separate controlled instruments: FrontierHarness Terminal-Bench 4.0 and Harness Leaderboard DeepSWE. On `Harnesses only`, select an instrument and then one of its control-model slices. Every chart, card, pass rate, cost, sample count and time belongs to the selected instrument. In the stacks view, only VulcanBench SWE and SWE-Atlas-QnA contribute to the composite.
- `FrontierCatalogue.tsx` renders a fixed-column (`table-fixed` `<colgroup>`, single-line `whitespace-nowrap` headers, zero content-driven column jitter) 5-column table (`Model` or `Harness + model` on Harnesses, `Provider`, `Score`, `Cost / task`, `Input / output ($/1M)`) supporting simultaneous multi-row expansion (`expandedConfigIds`, matching fixed-column multi-row expansion in `HarnessesView.tsx` and `PrunedCatalogue.tsx`) with expandable raw benchmark tiles showing each benchmark's converted score (`50 + 15 * (zScore - raschDelta)`) and weight (`% weight`), a plain-English explanation of the shared score scale and Morris EB pull toward the domain average (`50 + 15 * priorMean`), and an explicit inferred-cost note whenever `costImputed` is true. Every `<tr>` in `FrontierCatalogue.tsx`, `HarnessesView.tsx`, and `PrunedCatalogue.tsx` uses explicit `border-b border-[var(--border)] last:border-b-0` and `transition-[background-color]` (never `divide-y` + `transition-colors`). `PrunedCatalogue.tsx` collapses dominated models behind `<Activity>`, expands individual pruned rows on click (including direct chart pruned-marker clicks via `onSelectPrunedConfig` and `#pruned-*` anchors), and leads with harness names when `isHarnessDomain` is active. Never call `setState` during render for navigation/slice animations (`ProChoiceGallery.tsx`, `HarnessesView.tsx`).
- `EpochMonographDrawer.tsx` uses crisp, jargon-free human prose with consistent terminology (`model`, `provider`, `benchmark`, `score`, `weight`, `cost`), documents the two independent controlled harness instruments, distinguishes `"Discontinued"`, `"No public API price"`, `"No measured harness cost"` (Harnesses only), and `"High uncertainty"` (`shrinkageFactor < 0.50`) in held-out lists, never mentions harnesses on non-Harnesses domains, lists the source for the selected controlled instrument, omits the crowd-arena filter on non-Arena domains, never defaults missing audits to `"Healthy"`, and scopes `Data sources` strictly to the active view. Derive benchmark coverage from scored configurations and report feed-removal retention alongside matched-cohort rank agreement.

## Chart and keyboard accessibility (`src/components/ParetoPlate.tsx`, `src/components/HarnessesView.tsx`)

- Plot **solid direct lines between consecutive Pareto frontier points** (zero under-line shaded area fills on EVERY domain, single-curve or multi-curve) on a pure `var(--bg)` fixed-height SVG container initialized at desktop column width `1058` with `useLayoutEffect` pre-paint sizing (zero CLS and zero post-load expand), static-width `scaleX` `<clipPath>` reveal keyed ONLY to domain/regime/slice changes, regime-anchored axis bounds across ALL providers (including all pruned points when Pruned is active) so toggling providers on/off NEVER re-renders or shifts untouched provider curves, per-provider `<AnimatePresence initial={false}><motion.g>` + `<motion.path>` symmetric `pathLength` + `opacity` appear/disappear animations on toggle, interactive hover-focus curve isolation (dims others to 14% to 16%), matching 24px hover and click nearest-neighbor hit radii on both `ParetoPlate.tsx` and `HarnessesView.tsx`, and uniform static markers with `var(--bg)` strokes (ZERO point highlight halo rings and ZERO marker-size jumps on hover, selection, or scrub). Clicking any Pareto verdict plate or chart point naturally expands and scrolls the corresponding leaderboard table row to the vertical center of the viewport (`scrollIntoView({ block: "center" })`). Top Pareto verdict plates (`VerdictPlacards.tsx`, top harness picks in `HarnessesView.tsx`, and Home domain plates in `HomeView.tsx`) use elevated white `var(--surface)` (`#ffffff` light / `#211f1c` dark).
- Hovering or focusing a point renders ONLY a compact `pointer-events-none` single-line pill (`[Marker] Harness + Model · Effort` on Harnesses, or `[Marker] Model Name · Effort` elsewhere) placed cleanly above or below the point so neighboring points stay unobstructed. On `Harness + model`, frontier/pruned marker `aria-label` and spend-cap `<output aria-live="polite">` announcements MUST also lead with the harness name.
- The spend-cap readout bar below the chart is a fixed-height (`h-10`) zero-scrollbar bar (`overflow-hidden`) displaying the top qualifying picks responsively (1 on mobile, 2 on `sm`, 3 on `lg+` with `+N` overflow indicator; full list announced in `<output aria-live="polite">`), and uses `ceilSpendCapValue` for BOTH display and filtering so one displayed cap never gives two different answers. Clicking the instruction text does nothing (no cap-0 / `Clear` trigger); keyboard `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`Home`/`End` step strictly across real frontier model costs. Global `Escape` listeners ignore events while any `[role="dialog"]` is open.
- Account for `xMidYMid meet` letterboxing in `svgElementToRawSvg`, pair `r={10}` transparent click targets with Euclidean nearest-neighbor disambiguation, avoid empty-array re-allocations on `pointermove`, and track `focusedNode` independently from pointer hover (WCAG 1.4.13).
